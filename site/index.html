The model got fast. Now you're the bottleneck.

Run a few agents in terminals and your day turns into babysitting. Cockpit takes that job off your plate.

Claude Code in terminals

  • A session sits idle for 20 minutes because its "Allow?" scrolled away.
  • A production DROP TABLE scrolls past like any other command.
  • Every switch back means minutes of rereading.
  • The plan lives somewhere in the chat history.

Claude Code in Cockpit

  • Everything waiting on you sits in one queue, and you know the moment it arrives.
  • Risky commands stop in red, showing exactly what will run and where.
  • Each session tells you where it is with a plan and a change map.
  • Plans are a one-page map you can mark up and build from.

Less reading. Faster decisions.

Reading is the tiring part. A terminal makes you rebuild the context for every decision. Cockpit brings it with the question and leaves everything else out.

Only the decision

  • The question, the options and their trade-offs. You don't scroll back through 400 lines to see why it's asking.

Only the risk

  • The exact command, where it runs, and how hard it is to undo, highlighted before you press a key.

Only what changed

  • The plan's progress and the files it touched, grouped by folder. Auth and migrations are flagged.

One workflow, start to finish.

Here's what a morning with eight sessions looks like.

1

Everything that needs you, in one queue.

Questions, permissions and plan approvals from every session, oldest first. Press 1–3 to pick, A to allow, D to deny. The session carries on by itself.

2

Catch the command that sounds harmless.

Claude says "drop the old invoices table." The card shows the rest: it's production, and it's CASCADE. Press D, and Claude comes back with a safer plan instead of a restore from backup.

3

Know where every session stands without reading the transcript.

Each session has a plan with live progress and a change map grouped by folder, with auth and migrations flagged.

4

Plans you can read in ten seconds.

Ask Claude to plan before it codes and you get a one-page map: goal, scope, design, risks and open questions, each drawn the way it reads best. Mark it up, send every note at once, then hit Build from doc.

5

Know which session needs you next.

The timeline shows what every session did, what it does next, and who will need you in the next 15 minutes.

6

Review pull requests one finding at a time.

The riskiest snippets come first, with the findings ranked. Press one key to note a finding and it becomes a review comment. Address mode does the reverse and turns reviewer threads into fixes.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Cockpit is MIT licensed. Your sessions use your existing Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or API), exactly as they do in a terminal.

Do my skills, commands, hooks and settings still work?

Yes. Cockpit runs your real Claude Code with your real setup. Workspaces keep memory and history separate per client while sharing your skills and settings.

What if a card gets something wrong?

The full transcript is one key away (⌘R), and every approval shows the exact command before it runs.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. Cockpit is an independent open-source project built on Anthropic's public Agent SDK. "Claude" and "Claude Code" are trademarks of Anthropic.

Can I build it or contribute?

Please do: git clone https://github.com/getcockpit/cockpit && ./scripts/dev.sh. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
